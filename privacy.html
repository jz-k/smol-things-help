privacy policy

smol things · effective 19 October 2026

the short version

smol things does not collect your data. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

what the app stores

The things you add, how they felt, and your settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account, through Apple. We cannot see it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

notifications

Reminders, check-ins and nudges are scheduled on your device. They do not go through any server of ours.

tips

If you leave a tip, Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your payment details.

Apple Watch and widgets

The Watch app and the widget read the same data from your devices. Nothing is sent to us.

children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

get help

Email smol.things.help@gmail.com. Tell us what you saw, and which device and iOS version you use.

common questions

Why only three things? The limit is the point. Three things ask what you can do today.

How do I sync between devices? Turn on iCloud sync in the app's settings. Your devices must use the same iCloud account.

How do I stop notifications? Turn off the reminder, check-in or nudge in the app's settings. You can also rest for the day, and no notifications come while you rest.

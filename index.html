common questions

Why only three things?

This is not an app for every task. This is a quiet place for you to hold a few things close to you. It is for focus, not noise.

How do I sync between devices?

Turn on iCloud sync in the app's settings. Your devices must use the same iCloud account.

How do I stop notifications?

Turn off the reminder, check-in or nudge in the app's settings. You can also rest for the day. Notifications will rest with you.
